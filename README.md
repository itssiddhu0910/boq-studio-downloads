# BOQ Studio — Windows Desktop Edition

Commercial Bill of Quantities (BOQ), Margin Protection, Composite Assemblies, GST Quotation & Licensing Suite for Interior Designers, MEP/HVAC Contractors & Solar EPC Installers.

## Download for Windows (64-bit)

👉 **[Click here to download BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe (v2.0.1)](https://github.com/itssiddhu0910/boq-studio-downloads/releases/latest/download/BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe)**

### Installation & 14-Day Free Trial
1. Download and run **`BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe`**.
   - If Windows SmartScreen appears (*"Windows protected your PC"*), click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
2. Open **BOQ Studio** from your Desktop or Start Menu.
3. Your **14-Day Keyless Free Trial** starts automatically — click directly into **Overview**, **Catalogue**, **BOQs**, or **Quotations** to begin estimating right away.

### Activating a 1-Year Annual License
1. Open **License & device** in the left sidebar at any time (no need to wait for the trial to end).
2. Click **Copy ID** next to your **Composite Hardware ID** (`BOQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`), or click **Buy 1-Year Key on WhatsApp (+91 7012609030)** to send your Hardware ID automatically.
3. Paste your signed **1-Year License Key** into **Paste Signed 1-Year License Key** and click **Activate 1-Year License Key** to unlock 365 days and remove trial PDF watermarks.
