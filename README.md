# BOQ Studio — Windows Desktop Edition

Commercial Bill of Quantities (BOQ), Catalogue, and Quotation Management Suite.

## Download for Windows (64-bit)

👉 **[Click here to download BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe (v0.1.0)](https://github.com/itssiddhu0910/boq-studio-downloads/releases/latest/download/BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe)**

### Installation & Activation Steps
1. Download and run **`BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe`**.
   - If Windows SmartScreen appears (*"Windows protected your PC"*), click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
2. Open **BOQ Studio** from your Desktop or Start Menu.
3. On the **License** screen, click **Copy** next to your **Device fingerprint** (64-character Device ID) and send it to your administrator.
4. Once you receive your signed **License Token**, click **"Have a signed license token?"**, paste the token, and click **Save token** to unlock the workspace.
