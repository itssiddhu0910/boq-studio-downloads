### Installation, EULA & First Launch
1. Download and run **`BOQ-Studio-Setup.exe`** (or **`BOQ-Studio-Setup-v2.1.2.exe`**).
   - If Windows SmartScreen appears (*"Windows protected your PC"*), click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
2. Review the **End-User License Agreement (EULA) & Terms of Service**, check **"I accept the terms in the License Agreement"**, and click **Install**.
3. Open **BOQ Studio** from your Desktop or Start Menu and complete the one-time first-launch EULA confirmation to unlock the workspace.
4. For existing users upgrading from `v2.1.1` or earlier, the update is **100% non-destructive** — your local SQLite data, projects, Hardware ID, and active license are preserved automatically.
5. You can review the full license terms and consent record anytime under **Settings → Terms & Legal**.

### 14-Day Free Trial & 1-Year Annual License
1. New installations start a **14-Day Keyless Free Trial** automatically.
2. To activate a **1-Year Annual License**, open **License & device** in the left sidebar at any time.
3. Click **Copy ID** next to your **Composite Hardware ID** (`BOQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`), or click **Buy 1-Year Key on WhatsApp (+91 7012609030)** to send your Hardware ID automatically.
4. Paste your signed **1-Year License Key** and click **Activate 1-Year License Key** to unlock 365 days and remove trial PDF watermarks.
